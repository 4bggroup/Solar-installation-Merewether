# Electric Coast — asset notes

All six photographs were newly generated for this design using the built-in image generation tool, with natural Australian residential and documentary photography briefs. None of the prior design's photographs is included. The people are new illustrative characters.

The full prompt set is included in `image-prompts.json`.

WebP files and smaller responsive variants are used by the website. The original PNG masters are included separately in the downloadable ZIP for editing. Source dimensions are not described as 4K. Scenes are visual illustrations and are not evidence of real business work or exact site geography.

The logo, sun motif and conceptual energy illustration are code-native SVGs. They contain no third-party logos or product claims. The energy illustration is not an electrical diagram or installation guide.

Local fonts: URW Gothic Demi for display, Nimbus Sans for body text and Nimbus Mono PS for utility labels, converted to WOFF from the installed URW Base35 font distribution. The original distribution copyright and licence text is included as `assets/fonts/FONT-LICENCE.txt`.
